Which artists kept making great music as they aged?

Matter of Records

Music careers rated album by album. Critics, fans, sales and chart peaks for more than a hundred artists, side by side.

Every studio album by 118 artists, 1,528 albums in all, scored the same way on four measures: what critics said, what fans say now, what sold and streamed, and how high it charted.

Each artist gets a chart of their whole career, sleeve by sleeve, plus a timeline view. Put two artists head to head, compare up to four, or read the Top 20 Ever on each measure. Anyone can request an artist who isn't in the crate yet; requests are researched, scored and added automatically, usually within minutes.

Critic scores come from Metacritic, then AllMusic, then the album's Wikipedia review box. Nothing is made up: when a score doesn't exist, it stays blank.
