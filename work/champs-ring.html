Which teams have stayed on top the longest?

Champs Ring

Every playoff as a radial bracket: champion at the center, first round outside. Drawn at Hyperakt in 2012, revived in 2026 as fine-art prints.

The idea started with a poster for the 2010 World Cup, printed through Kickstarter. In 2012 it grew into The Champions Ring, a set of 427 brackets covering the history of the major leagues, the Champions League, the Euro and the World Cup.

It ran on the front page of the Berliner Morgenpost, in the Graphis Poster Annual and in exhibition at the Cité du Design, and Deadspin named it one of the best sports infographics of 2012.

In 2026 the system was redrawn and relaunched as Champs Ring, fine-art prints in White and Black editions, with history prints that hold every season of a league on one sheet.
