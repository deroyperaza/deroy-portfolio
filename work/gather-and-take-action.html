Gather and Take Action

An open-world satirical organizing game set in a fictional Brooklyn. Grand Theft Auto energy, except the crimes are mutual aid.

You start fresh out of jury duty in a fictional Brooklyn, with a community fridge to stock.

The map is 9 by 7 blocks of Brooklyn in real-world order, across ten neighborhoods from Downtown to Coney Island, with about 170 pedestrians, traffic, cyclists, bike docks and street vendors. Missions are about the everyday work of organizing, and a journal tracks the movement you build: solidarity, attention and trust.

Built for the browser, on desktop and phone, with touch controls.
