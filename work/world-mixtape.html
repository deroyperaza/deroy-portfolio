World Mixtape

The world's local music, decade by decade. Tap a country on the map and travel its sound from the 1920s to now, local artists only.

A map-based, decade-by-decade library of local popular music. Pick a country and an era and hear what people there were actually making and listening to, from the early 1900s to now. Every track is made by people from that place, natives and diaspora, never imports.

Each country is wrapped in context: photos, an outline map, key facts, its most important people and a plain-spoken history timeline. A song becomes a way into a place. Genre and popularity atlases map the whole catalog, and four daily games turn it into a quiz.

It grows out of two Hyperakt projects, The Refugee Project and On the Grid, both worldwide explorations built to help people understand context, history and perspective.
