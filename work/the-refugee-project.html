The Refugee Project

An interactive map of refugee migrations since 1975, built from UN data. Made at Hyperakt with Ekene Ijeoma; Gold, Information is Beautiful Awards 2014.

Choose a year and see where people fled from, or switch to asylum and see where they found refuge. Each major crisis is paired with a short story explaining why people left.

Hyperakt made it with Ekene Ijeoma and launched it in January 2014. It won Gold for Interactive at the 2014 Information is Beautiful Awards and Silver at Malofiej 22, was included in MoMA's Design and Violence and the Design Museum's Designs of the Year, and has been shown in museums in Queens, Los Angeles and Hamburg. It has been updated through 2024.
