What does a creative neighborhood look like, in cities big and small?

On the Grid

A designer's guide to neighborhood gems. Started in Gowanus at Hyperakt, it grew to 534 guides in 114 cities, each curated by local creatives.

Hyperakt launched it in April 2015 with Gowanus, Brooklyn, the studio's own neighborhood, then invited creative studios around the world to curate theirs.

Each guide was made by a local curator: they picked the places, shot a photo essay of each and wrote the reviews, and chose a color for their neighborhood. It grew to 534 guides in 114 cities and 43 countries, 11,000 places and 50,000 original photos, seen by 1.5 million visitors.

The system ran from the screen into the world: cubes, posters, notebooks, cards and starter kits for curators. On the Grid stopped updating in 2020 and stays online.
